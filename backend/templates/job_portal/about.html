Enterprise Workforce OS

Enterprise-Grade Human Capital Management

Nexus Workforce OS is an enterprise Human Capital Management and workforce intelligence platform developed by Nexus Global Technologies Inc. Engineered for organizations that demand precision, security, and scalability across global operations.

Access Portal OpenAPI Specs

Unified Architecture

Combines employee records, real-time biometric time-clocking, multi-level leave approvals, fixed-point payroll, and candidate recruitment into a single pane of glass.

Enterprise Security

Rigorous role-based access control (RBAC), encrypted credential handling, session integrity, and comprehensive audit logs designed to meet corporate compliance standards.

Decoupled REST API

Built on Django Ninja and Python 3.11 with typed schemas, asynchronous workers (Celery + Redis), and a modern React 19 single-page application.
